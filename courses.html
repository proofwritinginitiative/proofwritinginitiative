CURRENT COURSE

Communication of Mathematical Reasoning

FREE

Mathematics is not only about getting the right answer. Mathematicians must also be able to explain why an answer is correct.

In this introductory course, students learn the foundations of mathematical proof and discover how to communicate mathematical ideas clearly and rigorously.

Through examples, discussions, and guided practice, students develop the skills needed to construct convincing mathematical arguments.

View Class Details →
Audience Middle school mathematicians
Cost Free
Format Online
Experience No proof experience required

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Build the foundations of proof.

Students will learn both how mathematical proofs work and how to communicate their own reasoning effectively.

01

Mathematical Arguments

Understand what makes a mathematical argument convincing, logical, and complete.

02

Proof Structure

Learn how definitions, assumptions, statements, and conclusions work together in a proof.

03

Clear Communication

Practice explaining mathematical ideas using precise language and well-organized reasoning.

04

Critical Thinking

Develop the ability to question assumptions, identify gaps, and evaluate mathematical arguments.

COURSE STRUCTURE

Learn by doing mathematics.

Our courses are designed around active participation. Students are encouraged to ask questions, work through challenging problems, and explain their reasoning.

01

Learn a new idea

Each lesson introduces a mathematical concept through accessible examples and discussion.

02

Work through problems

Students apply new ideas to problems that require careful mathematical reasoning.

03

Write and communicate

Students practice turning their mathematical thoughts into clear written arguments.

04

Revise your reasoning

Students learn to identify weaknesses in their arguments and improve their mathematical writing.
