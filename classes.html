CURRENT CLASS

Communication of Mathematical Reasoning

FREE

This introductory course is designed for middle school students who want to explore mathematics beyond simply finding answers.

Students will learn how mathematicians construct arguments, use definitions, identify assumptions, and communicate conclusions.

No previous experience with formal mathematical proofs is required.

WHAT WE COVER

From mathematical ideas to written proofs.

Students gradually build the vocabulary and reasoning skills needed to write their own mathematical arguments.

01

Definitions

Learn why precise definitions are essential to mathematical reasoning.

02

Logical Reasoning

Practice connecting statements and drawing conclusions from mathematical information.

03

Proof Techniques

Explore fundamental approaches mathematicians use to establish that statements are true.

04

Mathematical Writing

Learn how to organize and communicate a proof so that the reasoning is clear to a reader.

THE STUDENT EXPERIENCE

A classroom built around reasoning.

Students are encouraged to participate, ask questions, test ideas, and learn from one another.

01

Discussion

Explore mathematical questions together and discuss different approaches.

02

Guided Practice

Work through examples with guidance before tackling problems independently.

03

Proof Writing

Put your reasoning into writing and practice constructing complete mathematical arguments.

04

Feedback

Learn how to recognize gaps in your reasoning and make your mathematical writing stronger.
