WHAT TO KNOW

Before you begin, know what to expect.

Mathematical proof may be new to you. That's okay. Our courses are designed to introduce proof writing step by step.

BEFORE THE COURSE

You don't need to already know how to prove things.

The most important requirement is curiosity. We will introduce the language, structure, and habits of mathematical proof during the course.

01

You should enjoy mathematics.

You do not need to be an expert mathematician. You should simply be interested in exploring problems and understanding why things work.

02

You should be willing to think deeply.

Proofs sometimes require several attempts. Students should be comfortable spending time with a difficult problem.

03

You will make mistakes.

Mistakes are an important part of learning mathematics. We use them as opportunities to improve our reasoning.

04

You will learn to explain your ideas.

A major part of proof writing is communication. Students will practice turning their thoughts into clear mathematical arguments.

A DIFFERENT WAY TO THINK

Finding an answer is only the beginning.

In many mathematics classes, students are primarily asked to find an answer. Proof-based mathematics asks a different question: how do you know your answer is correct?

01

Ask why.

Don't stop after finding a pattern. Ask why that pattern must always be true.

02

Examine assumptions.

Learn to identify exactly what a problem gives you and what you are allowed to use.

03

Build an argument.

Connect mathematical ideas together so that each step follows logically from the previous one.

04

Communicate clearly.

Write your reasoning so that another person can understand and verify it.

READY TO LEARN?

You don't need to be a proof expert.

You just need to be willing to ask questions, explore ideas, and explain your reasoning.
