OUR MISSION

Making proof-based mathematics accessible to young students.

We believe students should have the opportunity to explore mathematics beyond the standard curriculum.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Young students are capable of more mathematical reasoning than they are often given the opportunity to explore.

Proof-based mathematics can seem intimidating because it is often introduced using formal language and abstract ideas. We want to make those ideas approachable without removing their depth.

Our courses are designed to challenge students while providing the support they need to develop confidence as mathematical thinkers.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What guides our work.

Every course and learning experience we create is built around a few simple principles.

01

Access

Advanced mathematical ideas should not be limited to students who already have access to specialized programs.

02

Rigor

Making mathematics accessible does not mean making it superficial. Students deserve meaningful challenges.

03

Curiosity

Students should have space to explore questions, make conjectures, and discover ideas.

04

Communication

Understanding mathematics includes being able to explain mathematical reasoning clearly.

OUR GOAL

We want students to leave with more than a collection of techniques.

The long-term goal is to help students become more independent, confident, and thoughtful mathematical thinkers.

01

Confidence

Students become more comfortable approaching unfamiliar mathematical problems.

02

Independence

Students learn strategies for exploring problems without immediately relying on an answer.

03

Precision

Students learn why definitions, assumptions, and carefully chosen language matter.

04

A love of mathematics

Most importantly, we want students to discover the satisfaction that comes from understanding why something is true.
