ABOUT US

Helping young mathematicians think more deeply.

The Proof Writing Initiative provides free opportunities for students to explore rigorous, proof-based mathematics.

WHO WE ARE

Mathematics should be something students can explore, not just something they have to finish.

We created the Proof Writing Initiative to give young students an opportunity to encounter mathematical ideas that are often introduced much later in their education.

01

Curious

We encourage students to ask questions and look beyond the first solution to a problem.

02

Rigorous

We teach students to support mathematical claims with careful reasoning and evidence.

03

Accessible

We believe advanced mathematical ideas can be introduced in ways that are approachable for young students.

OUR APPROACH

We teach students to become mathematical thinkers.

Our goal is not simply to teach students a collection of proof techniques. We want them to develop habits of reasoning that remain useful long after a course ends.

01

Ask questions

Good mathematics begins with curiosity. Students are encouraged to question ideas and investigate patterns.

02

Explore possibilities

Students test examples, make conjectures, and search for ways to understand a problem.

03

Justify conclusions

Students learn the difference between believing something is true and proving it.

04

Communicate ideas

Students learn to express mathematical reasoning clearly and precisely.

WHY PROOF?

Because learning to prove something is learning how to know something.

Proof teaches students to slow down, examine assumptions, connect ideas, and communicate conclusions. These are valuable mathematical skills—and valuable intellectual skills beyond mathematics as well.
